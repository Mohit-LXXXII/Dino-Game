--- DESCRIPTION ---
A complete 16x16 pixel art tileset for 2D side-scroller and platformer games. 
Perfect for Metroidvania or Action-Platformer projects.

--- CONTENTS ---
- 1 x Master Tileset Sprite Sheet (256x256px, PNG format with transparency)
  - 9-Slice Ground Tiles
  - Slope and Ledge Tiles
  - Modular Trees & Canopies
  - Decorative Props (Ferns, Flowers, Vines)
- 5 x Seamless Parallax Background Layers (384x216px, PNG format)

--- TECHNICAL INFORMATION ---
- Grid Size: 16x16 pixels
- Format: PNG (Alpha Transparency)
